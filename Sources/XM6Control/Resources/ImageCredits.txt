- xm5.png — Sony WH-1000XM5 silver product photo, via Spark NZ

- No XM4 photo is bundled: no high-resolution silver shot could be retrieved.
  XM4 owners see the built-in vector illustration, or can drop their own
  `WH-1000XM4.png` / `xm4.png` into `~/Library/Application Support/XM Control/`.

These are Sony product photos, shown for identification of the hardware this
app controls. They are NOT covered by the MIT license of the code. If you are
the rights holder and want them removed, open an issue and they will go.

To use your own local photo (never redistributed): place
`WH-1000XM4.png`, `WH-1000XM5.png` or `WH-1000XM6.png` (or `headphones.png`)
in `~/Library/Application Support/XM Control/` and relaunch.

The app icon is generated locally by `Scripts/make_icon.swift` (system symbol,
no third-party artwork). Code: MIT license, see LICENSE (original attribution
to Rui Martins kept).
